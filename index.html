Our name我們的名字

Every drop of rain ends up in the sea.每一滴雨，最後都會回到海裡

“Aetherain” joins the sky and the rain. 蔚霖, our Chinese name, means a blessing of rain that reaches every sea.Aetherain 結合了天空與雨水。「蔚霖」的意思，是甘霖降下，澤被四海。

We are a Taiwan company founded in 2026 by people who have spent their careers on the water — with owners, crews, marinas and builders. We treat every project the way we treat the sea: with care for the details, and respect for what we leave behind.我們是2026年在台灣成立的公司，團隊長年在海上工作，熟悉船主、船員、碼頭與造船廠。我們對待每個案子，就像對待大海一樣：在意細節，也在意我們留下了什麼。

What we do我們的業務

Three ways we work with the sea我們和大海合作的三種方式

Engineering is our foundation. Advisory and brand partnerships grow from the same owner and marina network.海洋工程是我們的根本。顧問服務與品牌合作，則來自同一個船主與碼頭網絡。

01

Floating docks & marine engineering浮動碼頭與海洋工程

Floating pontoons, marinas and waterfront structures for harbours, fishing ports and lakes — from project packaging and supplier coordination to supervision on site.為港口、漁港與湖泊規劃浮筒、浮動碼頭與水上設施，從案子整合、供應商協調，到現場監造。

02

Yacht advisory遊艇顧問

Owner-side guidance on buying, berthing, handover and day-to-day yacht management in Taiwan, based on years of private yacht management.站在船主這一方，提供購船、停泊、交船與日常管理的建議，來自多年私人遊艇管理的實務經驗。

03

European marine brands for Taiwan歐洲海洋品牌引進

We introduce selected yachts, yacht-care products and onboard textiles to Taiwanese owners and dealers, with local berthing and owner relationships behind every introduction.把精選的遊艇、遊艇保養用品與船上織品介紹給台灣的船主與經銷商，每一次引進，背後都有在地碼頭與船主關係支持。

For brand partners給品牌夥伴

A brand needs more than a showroom. It needs a place to berth.品牌需要的不只是展示間，還需要一個能停靠的地方

  • Bilingual communication and on-site hosting for visiting partners.中英雙語溝通，並可在台灣接待來訪夥伴。
  • Our own short-video channel, “Angela at Sea”, written for buyers and experienced sailors.自有短影音頻道「Angela在海上」，為買家與資深玩家而寫。
