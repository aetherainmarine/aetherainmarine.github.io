We treat every project the way we treat the sea: with care for the details, and respect for what we leave behind.我們是2026年在台灣成立的公司，團隊長年在海上工作，熟悉船主、船員、碼頭與造船廠。我們對待每個案子，就像對待大海一樣：在意細節，也在意我們留下了什麼。

What we do我們的業務

Three ways we work with the sea我們和大海合作的三種方式

01

Floating docks & marine engineering浮動碼頭與海洋工程

Floating pontoons, marinas and waterfront structures for harbours, fishing ports and lakes — from project packaging and supplier coordination to supervision on site.為港口、漁港與湖泊規劃浮筒、浮動碼頭與水上設施，從案子整合、供應商協調，到現場監造。

02

Yacht advisory遊艇顧問

Owner-side guidance on buying, berthing, handover and day-to-day yacht management in Taiwan, based on years of private yacht management.站在船主這一方，提供購船、停泊、交船與日常管理的建議，來自多年私人遊艇管理的實務經驗。

03

European marine brands for Taiwan歐洲海洋品牌引進

We introduce selected yachts, yacht-care products and onboard textiles to Taiwanese owners and dealers, with local berthing and owner relationships behind every introduction.把精選的遊艇、遊艇保養用品與船上織品介紹給台灣的船主與經銷商，每一次引進，背後都有在地碼頭與船主關係支持。
